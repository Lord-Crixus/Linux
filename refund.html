Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Payments are one-time

Every payment is for a single week or month of access. We never charge you again automatically.

When we will refund or fix a payment

If you paid and did not get access, or you were charged twice, message us on Telegram within 7 days with your payment receipt. We will either give you access or refund you.

When we do not refund

We do not refund because a pick lost. Predictions are not guarantees. Once you have been given access, the payment is not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the method you paid with, through Paystack. Timing depends on your bank or mobile money provider.
