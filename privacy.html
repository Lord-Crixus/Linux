Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What we collect

When you pay, Paystack collects your name, email and payment details. We receive your name, email, Telegram username and payment confirmation. We do not see or store your card number or M-Pesa PIN.

How we use it

We use it to confirm your payment, give you access to the Telegram channel, answer your messages and keep payment records.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your payment is processed by Paystack, and your chats happen on Telegram, so their privacy policies also apply.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses essential cookies to work. Optional analytics cookies are used only if you accept them.

Your choices

You can ask us on Telegram to see or delete the information we hold about you, except records we must keep for payment reasons.
